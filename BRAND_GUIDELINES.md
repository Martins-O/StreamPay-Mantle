## 🎭 Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Attributes
- **Professional** but not stuffy
- **Technical** but not jargon-heavy
- **Forward-thinking** but grounded in reality
- **Confident** but not arrogant
- **Helpful** but not patronizing

### Do's
✅ Use active voice: "Liquifi transforms revenue" not "Revenue is transformed"
✅ Be specific: "8-12% APY" not "high yields"
✅ Explain benefits: "Instant liquidity means no more waiting 60 days for payment"
✅ Use analogies: "Like Spotify for payments—continuous, not batch"
✅ Show real numbers: "$50k monthly revenue → $40k instant capital"

### Don'ts
❌ Don't use crypto jargon unnecessarily: Say "earn yield" not "farm APR"
❌ Don't oversell: Avoid "revolutionary," "game-changing," "disrupting"
❌ Don't be vague: Not "great returns" but "10% APY"
❌ Don't use all-caps: "STREAM" → "Stream"
❌ Don't use excessive emojis in formal contexts

### Example Content

**Bad:**
"🚀 LIQUIFI IS REVOLUTIONIZING DEFI!!! 💰 GET INSANE YIELDS NOW!!!"

**Good:**
"Liquifi turns future revenue into instant capital. Businesses get liquidity today, investors earn 8-12% APY from real cashflows."

## 🎬 Motion & Animation

### Loading States
- Use flowing water ripple effect
- Color: Cyan gradient
- Duration: 1.5-2 seconds loop

### Success States
- Brief cyan flash
- Checkmark with scale-in animation
- Optional: Particle burst effect

### Error States
- Shake animation (2-3 times)
- Red glow
- No harsh transitions

### Transitions
- Duration: 200-300ms
- Easing: ease-in-out or cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
- Avoid jarring jumps—always animate position changes
